## Short description (≤80)

## Full description (≤4000)

Welcome to Bloop World, the minigame hub by TEAM Ross! One app packed with quick, colorful games for all ages.

6 GAMES
• Bloop Orbit — one tap to hop from planet to planet. Nail PERFECT landings and dodge the asteroids!
• Spin Drop — spin the tower and dive through the gaps without touching the red.
• Moon Rally — speed over moon hills, pull off flips and upgrade your ride in the garage.
• Star Factory — build machines, collect stars and launch the TURBO Rocket before your rivals.
• Disaster Survival — climb, run and escape earthquakes, tsunamis, meteors and more!
• Bloop Band — form a band, play to the beat with friends and make your own songs in the studio.

A PET THAT TALKS BACK
Feed it, bathe it, cuddle it and play ball. Talk near your phone and it repeats you in a funny little voice — your voice stays only in the device's memory and is never recorded or sent.

HOMES AND PLANETS
Build and decorate your house and your planet with thousands of combinations. Visit friends, help them tidy up, throw parties with food fights and step into 3D mode (on supported devices).

COLLECT AND CREATE
• 300 collectible critters, with new ones every monthly season
• 3 monthly passes — Star, Home and Music — with free rewards and an optional Premium track
• Character creator: build your own Bloop piece by piece
• Skins, trails, auras and dances

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Championships with brackets and coin prizes
• Races and leaderboards
• Chat with safety features: automatic filter, Safe Mode with quick phrases only for players under 13, and mute, block and report buttons. House parties are emotes only.

Available in 12 languages. Online features (friends, parties, band with friends, chat, leaderboards) require an internet connection. Free to play; contains ads and optional in-app purchases. Ask a parent or guardian before buying.
