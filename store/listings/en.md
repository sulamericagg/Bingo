## Full description (≤4000)

Welcome to Bloop World, the minigame hub by TEAM Ross! One app packed with quick, colorful games made for all ages.

THE GAMES
• Bloop Orbit — one tap to hop from planet to planet. Nail PERFECT landings and dodge the asteroids!
• Spin Drop — spin the tower and dive through the gaps without touching the red.
• Moon Rally — speed over moon hills, pull off flips and upgrade your ride in the garage.
• Star Factory — build machines, collect stars and launch the TURBO Rocket before your rivals.
• Disaster survival mode — escape wacky catastrophes and be the last one standing!

A PET THAT TALKS BACK
Feed it, bathe it, cuddle it, decorate its room and play ball. Talk near your phone and it repeats you in a funny little voice — your voice stays only in the device's memory and is never recorded or sent.

COLLECT AND CREATE
• 300 collectible critters: 5 new ones every monthly season
• Monthly Star Pass with free rewards and an optional Premium track
• Character creator: build your own Bloop piece by piece
• Dozens of skins and trails, from common to mythic

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Championships with brackets and coin prizes
• Races and weekly leaderboards
• Chat with safety features: automatic filter, Safe Mode with quick phrases only for players under 13, and mute, block and report buttons

Available in 12 languages. Free to play; contains ads and optional in-app purchases. Ask a parent or guardian before buying.
